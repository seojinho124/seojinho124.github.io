Chemistry education

Teaching

Teaching has been an important part of my graduate training at UC Davis.

Teaching experience

Since 2023, I have supported undergraduate chemistry courses through discussion sections, problem solving, assessment, office hours, and individual student guidance.

My teaching experience includes explaining organic chemistry concepts such as reaction mechanisms, stereochemistry, spectroscopy, and structure–reactivity relationships.

Approach

I aim to make difficult ideas easier to reason through rather than reducing them to rules to memorize. Clear diagrams, explicit assumptions, and connections between representations help students build transferable chemical intuition.
