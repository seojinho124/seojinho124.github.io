Current Work

A record of the questions I have worked on, the tools I have developed, and the broader scientific directions that interest me.

Research experience

My graduate work has examined complex organic reaction mechanisms and the structural environments that shape them. This experience gave me a foundation for studying molecular behavior across electronic and biomolecular scales.

Electronic structure

DFT calculations, conformational searches, potential-energy surfaces, and reaction-energy profiles.

Molecular modeling

Protein–ligand docking, chemically informed constraints, biological electron and proton transfer, in silico mutation analysis, and conformational sampling.

Molecular dynamics

Ab initio molecular dynamics, empirical force-field simulations, enhanced sampling, and trajectory and conformational-ensemble analysis.

Computing

Python, Linux, high-performance computing, Gaussian, Rosetta, and related computational-chemistry tools.

Research interests

My interests extend broadly across computational and biophysical chemistry. I am especially interested in how molecular structure, electronic behavior, conformational dynamics, and local environments combine to control chemical and biological function.

Molecular structure & reactivity

Connecting electronic structure and mechanism to experimentally meaningful molecular behavior.

Proteins & molecular environments

Understanding how active sites, binding interactions, dynamics, solvent, and organized water affect function.

Spectroscopy & computation

Using calculated molecular properties to interpret or support magnetic-resonance and other structural measurements.

Integrated methods

Combining quantum chemistry, molecular modeling, simulation, and data analysis to answer multiscale questions.
