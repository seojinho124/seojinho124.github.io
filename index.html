Research focus

From electronic structure to biological function.

My work connects detailed reaction mechanisms with the three-dimensional environments that shape them. I use computation to ask not only what a molecule can do, but how an enzyme makes one pathway more likely than another.

01

Reaction mechanisms

Mapping intermediates, transition states, and energy profiles with density functional theory.

Enzyme active sites

Studying how protein structure, conformations, and organized water influence catalysis.

03

Molecular modeling

Building chemically informed models that connect intrinsic reactivity with enzyme selectivity.

Current work

Questions I am exploring.

My graduate research centers on the boundary between intrinsic carbocation chemistry and the constraints imposed by terpene synthase active sites.

Mechanism

Carbocation reaction pathways

Characterizing conformations, intermediates, transition structures, and competing routes on complex potential-energy surfaces.

Modeling

Mechanistically informed docking

Using reactive-state geometries and chemical constraints to examine whether proposed pathways are compatible with enzyme active sites.

Analysis

Structure and selectivity

Comparing docked ensembles through geometric filters, RMSD, interaction patterns, and statistical analysis.

Teaching
